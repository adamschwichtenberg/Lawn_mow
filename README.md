# Backyard Stripes

A photoreal-ish lawn mowing simulator in one self-contained `index.html` (Three.js r170 from the jsDelivr CDN; all textures, models and audio are procedural).

**Play:** open `index.html` in Chrome, Safari or Firefox (double-click works; you need an internet connection to load Three.js).

## Modes
- **Job**: mow the Hendersons' backyard to 95% coverage. The customer then inspects the lawn and gives you a 1–5 star rating and a payout. Par time is 2:30.
- **Zen**: endless mowing with no timer or rating. The grass regrows in about 4 minutes, and you choose the time of day.

## Controls
| Action | Desktop | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Drive / reverse | W / S or ↑ / ↓ | Left joystick (up / down) |
| Turn | A / D or ← / → | Left joystick (left / right) |
| Boost | Shift | Boost button |
| Lift deck (no cutting) | Space | Deck button |
| Refuel / sharpen (on the patio) | E | Action button |
| Cycle camera (chase / first person / top-down) | C | Camera button |
| Orbit camera | Mouse drag | Drag the right side of the screen |
| Pause | Esc | II button |

Tips: mow clean back-and-forth passes for the best stripe score. Lift the deck over the two little crests (or you'll scalp them), and near the flower bed, mulch rings and sprinkler heads. Watch out for Biscuit the dog.
